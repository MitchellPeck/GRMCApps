When the screen is on
Outside these hours the screen shows nothing at all — true black, with the video torn down rather than hidden. That alone costs nothing and works with any television. Actually cutting the panel's power is the card below.
No hours set yet.
Times are wall-clock in the app timezone and follow daylight saving. Add more than one row for a day to cover a morning and an evening; rows that touch or overlap are treated as one stretch, so the screen never blinks off between them. An Until earlier than From means the window runs past midnight.
Turning the TV on and off

Optional, and separate from blanking the picture. At each boundary above, the server can send one request on your network — to the television, a smart plug, or a hub. Leave both at Nothing and the screen simply goes black on time, which is the safe default.
