Voice recognition
The app learns each person’s voice from the names chosen under “4. Voices” and from meetings whose minutes have been written. It then recognises them in later recordings. Only a voice print (a list of numbers) is kept, never extra audio. Anyone’s voice can be forgotten from the People tab.
Transcription
Live and uploaded-audio transcription run on a self-hosted Whisper service on your own server — no API key, no per-minute cost, and audio never leaves your machine. Live transcription streams your mic in real time; use a good microphone for best results.
