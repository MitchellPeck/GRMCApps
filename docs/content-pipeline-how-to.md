# Content Pipeline: how to use it

**Where:** social.grmc.app/pipeline, or the **Content pipeline** tab in Social Posts.

The pipeline answers three questions: what are we posting, when, and what do
we need to capture to post it? Everything you see (dashboard, calendars,
board, event pages) is the same set of posts filtered different ways, so a
change made anywhere shows up everywhere.

## The pieces

- **Events and departments** group the work, for example *Kids Camp: GALAXY*
  (an event with dates) or *Youth Ministry* (ongoing). Every post series, and
  each weekly run (Grace Notes, Weekly blog, Podcast), has one automatically.
- **Posts** have a title, a publish date, platforms, an owner, a brief and a
  caption.
- **Media** is each thing a post needs captured: a photo, a video, an
  interview. Each has a gather date and the person capturing it.

## Every week

1. **Open the Dashboard.** The navy band lists what's missing. Click a number
   to see exactly which posts or media it means.
2. **Plan new posts.** Click **New post**, or the **+** on a calendar day.
   Only a title and a publish date are required. After saving, list the media
   it needs, or tick **No media needed** for a text-only post.
3. **Schedule captures.** Switch the Calendar to **Capture dates**. Media with
   no day waits in the tray underneath; drag it onto the day it will be
   gathered. Give it a person in the post's media list.
4. **Move posts along.** On the **Board**, drag a card to the next column or
   pick its status on the card.

## Recurring posts

Anything posted on a rhythm, like a weekly devotional or a monthly volunteer
thank-you, can repeat. Go to **Recurring → New recurring post** (or click
**Make it repeat** while creating a post). Choose weekly on certain days, every
few weeks, monthly on a date ("the 1st") or monthly on a weekday ("the last
Sunday"), and list any media to capture each time, for example *"Sanctuary
photo, 1 day before"*.

The next 8 weeks of posts appear on the calendar as ordinary posts, so each one
can have its own caption, media and owner. Paging the calendar further ahead
adds more.

- **Changing one post:** edit it and save with **This post only**.
- **Changing all of them:** pick **This and later posts in the series**, or
  open **Edit the series**. Posts someone has already drafted or finished are
  never overwritten.
- **Skipping a week:** open that post and click **Skip this date**.
- **Pausing or stopping:** pause it on the Recurring tab, or **End series** in
  its editor. Upcoming posts nobody started are removed; everything else stays.

The Wednesday Grace Notes, Saturday invite and Friday blog are already set up
this way, so their slots sit on the calendar weeks ahead. When the run drafts
a post, it fills that slot.

## The post editor

It's the same editor wherever you open a post, and a new post works just like
a saved one. You can list its media before you've even saved it.

**Captions.** Choose **Same everywhere** for one caption on every platform, or
**Different for each** for an Instagram, Facebook and X tab, each with its own
text. Each counter shows the length the way that platform counts it (X counts
any link as 23 characters) and turns red when you're over.

**Draft with Claude** writes from the title, brief and the event's notes. With
different captions, it writes one for each platform the way that platform
reads: link in bio on Instagram, the full link on Facebook, and 280 characters
at most on X. Review, then save.

**Instagram needs an image.** Ticking "No media needed" turns Instagram off, and
a text-only post can't be sent there.

**Send to Metricool** opens the send box with the date, time and platforms
filled in. With different captions, each platform's text is sent as its own
post. If the post has a file from Approvals, it's already chosen as the image.
Once it's sent, the post moves to Scheduled.

## Media

Each thing a post needs has a **Collected** box: tick it when you have it. No
link is needed. To attach the finished file, click **Choose from Approvals**
and pick anything that's been signed off, or paste a link to it instead. A
media item with its file attached shows as **Final file**.

## My work

The **My work** tab shows the posts you own and the media you're capturing. It
also lists captures nobody has taken yet; click **I'll take it** to claim one.

At the bottom is a link for your phone's calendar ("add calendar from URL" in
Apple or Google Calendar). Your capture days then appear there and update as
plans change. Anyone with the link can see the schedule, so keep it within the
team; **Reset the link** makes a new one and stops the old one working.

## Next year's event

On an event's page, **Duplicate** copies it with every post and media item to
new dates, for example Kids Camp 2026 into 2027. The copies start as ideas,
with nothing captured.

## Statuses

| Post | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Idea | A topic and a date, nothing more |
| Planned | Brief written and media listed |
| Drafted | Caption written (the weekly runs land here automatically) |
| Ready | Caption approved and all media captured |
| Scheduled | Queued in Metricool (set automatically when you send it from Social Posts) |
| Published | Live |
| Cancelled | Off the calendars, kept for history |

Media goes **Needed → Scheduled → Collected → Final file**. Setting a gather
date makes it Scheduled on its own; attaching the file from Approvals (or a
link) makes it Final. A post can't be marked **Ready** until all of its media
is collected; the editor tells you what is still missing.

## Good to know

- The filters at the top (event, platform, status, owner) stay set as you
  move between screens. **Clear filters** resets them.
- If media is set to be gathered after its post goes out, it turns red and
  shows up on the dashboard.
- Drafts from the Wednesday, Friday and Podcast runs, and from series, appear
  in the pipeline as **Drafted** on their date. Re-running a draft updates the
  same post instead of adding a second one.
- Files aren't uploaded here: pick them from Approvals, or paste a link.
